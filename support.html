Support

Habits: Streak Tracker

Email ratehabits@gmail.com, or use Settings › Feedback › Send feedback in the app. Every message is read.

How do I restore Premium on a new device?

Open Habits › Settings › Habits Premium and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription or free trial?

Open the iOS Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Habits, and tap Cancel Subscription. To avoid being charged after the 7-day free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

My habits aren't on my new iPhone or iPad

Habits syncs through iCloud. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on both devices and that iCloud Drive is on. Open the app and wait a minute; large histories can take a little longer. You can also use Settings › Back up and Restore from backup.

How do I use the Apple Watch app?

The Apple Watch app is part of Premium. Install it from the Watch app on your iPhone (App Store section), then open Habits on your watch. Today's habits appear and you can tick them off from your wrist.

Apple Health auto-complete isn't working

Open the iOS Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Habits and allow the data types you use. Then link the habit to a Health metric when you edit it.
