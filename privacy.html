Privacy Policy

Habits: Streak Tracker · Effective 16 November 2026

The short version

Habits does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. We run no servers, so we never receive your habits.

Where your data is stored

  • On your device. Your habits, check-ins, notes and moods are stored on your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.
  • In your own iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, Habits syncs your data through your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. Only you can access it; we cannot see it. You can turn this off in the iOS Settings app under your name › iCloud.
  • Backups you make. If you export a backup or CSV file, it goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Habits reads steps, workouts, sleep, water and mindful minutes from Apple Health to complete your habits automatically. Habits only reads this data on your device to update your habit progress. It never writes to Apple Health, never sends Health data to us or anyone else, and never uses it for advertising.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. You can manage or cancel a subscription in the iOS Settings app under your name › Subscriptions.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, iOS may give Habits a technical report, which is kept on your device. It is only sent to us if you choose to share it from Settings.

Email

If you email us (for example with "Send feedback"), we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Children

Habits does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
